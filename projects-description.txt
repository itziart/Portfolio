Assassin Elf First Realistic Character
This project marks a big step for me. It’s my first time pushing toward realism in a character, and
I’ve learned a lot throughout the process.
This character is based on a concept by BangkuART, which served as the visual foundation for
the project.
Neomorph
Created from a sphere, based on a concept by Russell Dongjun Lu.
This project helped me push my understanding of creature anatomy, proportions, and silhouette.
Crime Shoes
Based on a concept by notperera, in this project I wanted to challenge myself modelling
something less organic while also trying to give storytelling to a mere prop and with my first
handpainted project.
Dolfo
Stop Motion project executed by my classmates and I, the challenge was to create 9 cm figures
with Jovi Clay and being able to animate them. I designed, sculpted and animated every
character on this project.
Scary Stories to tell in the dark
Project based on the movie that was inspired by a book with different stories.
I merged 2 of them and designed the character based on it. It helped me understand better what
happens to your body while decomposing .
Beast Book
First time I laser cut a piece of porex which serve me as the base of this project that I later on
sculpted the details with epoxi resine which gave me 20 minutes to work on the different items
that assemble the book. It’s my own twist to the Beast Book of Harry Potter with some Jujutsu
Kaisen reference.
Magritte ft Twin Peaks
My first scene and big modeling project which make me learn the bases of 3D modelling and
Texturing in Maya. I wanted to create a dreamy environment, out of this world.
The Foot
3D Animation Project made from scratch which made us work under pressure and as a real
team. We learned the the animation pipeline and delivered a comic animation.
I designed the ants, build the storyboard, sculpted both of them, animated them and also did the
close up scene with textures included.
